Best Practices
--------------

.. important::

    **Always keep an eye on your terminal window**

    This is where progress for some processes (tiling, inference etc) is visible, and results for some modules (count labels, delete labels etc) are printed out.

.. important::

    **Your images should have "TEM contrast"**

    Raw images from SEM based volume EM approaches (FIB-SEM, SBF-SEM, SEM-AT) should be contrast inverted to look like conventional TEM images of heavy metal stained, resin embedded biological samples.
